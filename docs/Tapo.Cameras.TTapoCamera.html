Declaration

type TTapoCamera = class(TTapoDevice)

Description

Tapo pan/tilt camera: C210, C220, C225, C325WB, C520WS, TC40 and TC70.

Logs in over TPAP (HTTPS), or over AES SSL if Third-Party Compatibility is on. The camera's self-signed certificate is accepted.

Taking a snapshot is not supported here: open the MJPEG address from GetRtspStreamUrl with any RTSP-capable player or library.

Example: 

Cam := Client.Add(C210, '192.168.1.54');
Cam.PanTilt(10, 0);
for Preset in Cam.GetPresets do
  ListBox1.Items.Add(Preset.Name);
